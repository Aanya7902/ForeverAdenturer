Google Photos Privacy & Setup

Under Google's current privacy model, third-party apps never get silent background access to your entire library. You select only the specific travel photos inside an official Google popup.

Production Google Cloud Setup:
  1. Create a project on console.cloud.google.com.
  2. Enable Google Photos Picker API.
  3. Create an OAuth 2.0 Web Client ID with your Vercel URL in Authorized JavaScript Origins.
